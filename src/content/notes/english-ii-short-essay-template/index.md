## 段落结构--书信

**格式**:

Dear Li Ming/professor Smith/friend/Sir or Madam,

第一段(开头空两格)

第二段(开头空两格)

第三段(开头空两格)

私人:Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

公务：Truly yours,
Li Ming

**模板**:

第一段(2句话)：自我介绍+写信目的

1. I am ...我是...

2. I'm writing this letter to...我写这封信是...

第二段(4句话)：总起句+分层阐述

**总**：Provided below are my...以下是我的...
To begin with, ...首先，

In addition, ...另外，

Furthermore, ...此外，

第三段(2句话)：表示感谢+期待回信

* 建议信/邀请信/投诉信

    1. 我很高兴，如果你能采纳我的建议/接受我的邀请/尽快处理投诉
        
        I would be deeply grateful if...
 
* 感谢信/道歉信/祝贺信

1. 我将再次表达我对你的感谢/道歉/祝贺

Again, I would like to express my heartfelt ... to you! Please accept my ...

2. I'm looking forward to hearing from you at your earliest
convenience.

**备用词汇**:

1.建议信:动词:suggest, advise, sb.to do sth.

        名词: make/give some suggestions/advice/proposals for sb.

2.邀请信:动词:invite sb.to do sth.

        名词:send sb. an invitation to do sth.

3.感谢信:动词:thank you for (doing) sth.

        名词: express my sincere thanks/gratitude to you for (doing) sth.

4.道歉信:动词:apologize for (doing) sth.

        名词: make an apology for (doing) sth.

5.祝贺信:动词:congratulate sb.on/upon sth.

        名词: express/convey my congratulations to sb. on sth.
6.投诉信:动词:complain to sb. about sth.

        名词: make a complaint about sth.

7.公告/通知:announcement/notice

## 段落结构--告示/通知

Notice

\_\_\_(活动) will be held in \_\_\_(活动地点) on \_\_\_(活动日期).

The relevant procedures and arrangement of this activity are announced as follows. Firstly, this activity will focus on \_\_\_(主题). Moreover, \_\_\_(某人) will give an address at the beginning. **Last but not least**, all the participants andaudience are required to \_\_\_(出席要求)in the whole process.

We sincerely welcome you to attend this event at your convenience. Please contact us no later than \_\_\_(时间).

The Students' Union

December 20^{th}.2021
